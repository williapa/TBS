# 3D asset provenance

The MVP renderer uses project-owned procedural geometry assembled from Three.js primitives. No third-party models, textures, fonts, or binary assets are redistributed by this package.

`src/assets/modelManifest.ts` is the authoritative renderer manifest. Each entry identifies its procedural geometry and fallback behavior. Future GLB entries must record their source, author, license, and modification history here before they are committed.

The missile uses `src/assets/MissileModel.tsx`: a slim ivory projectile with a red pointed nose, dark collar bands, four swept solid tail fins, and a recessed exhaust nozzle. A slight lean and red casing marks keep the silhouette readable at board scale. Its fixed palette reflects neutral ownership, and it follows entity orientation and shared selection behavior. The manifest clears the nose if a health indicator is supplied. All geometry is project-authored without textures, external assets, or dependencies. Gameplay and persisted contracts are unchanged.
