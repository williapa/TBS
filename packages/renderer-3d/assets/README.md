# 3D asset provenance

The MVP renderer uses project-owned procedural geometry assembled from Three.js primitives. No third-party models, textures, fonts, or binary assets are redistributed by this package.

`src/assets/modelManifest.ts` is the authoritative renderer manifest. Each entry identifies its procedural geometry and fallback behavior. Future GLB entries must record their source, author, license, and modification history here before they are committed.

The nuke uses `src/assets/NukeModel.tsx`: a squat olive bomb with a rounded nose, yellow hazard bands, four dark tail fins, and yellow-and-black radiation badges on all four sides. Its fixed palette reflects the object's neutral ownership. It follows entity orientation and shared selection behavior, with manifest clearance above the nose if a health indicator is supplied. All geometry, including the radiation symbols, is project-authored from primitives without textures, fonts, external assets, or dependencies. Missile objects retain their existing fallback; gameplay and persisted contracts are unchanged.

The soldier uses `src/assets/SoldierModel.tsx`: a low-poly infantry miniature with a faceted helmet, recessed blue visor, armored vest and knee plates, staggered boots, canvas field pack, and a rifle held across the chest. Shoulder plates, a chest badge, and a helmet stripe carry the team color. It follows entity orientation and the shared selection, movement, and reduced-motion behavior, with its health bar above the helmet. This is a new project-authored design created from primitives without consulting the stashed draft or previously compiled soldier artifacts; it uses no external assets or dependencies. Other people retain their existing models or generic fallback.
