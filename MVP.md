# The Simplest-Possible Rules
- animals move 2
- people move 1
- vehicles move 3
- exclude water stuff
- flying = mountains only
- roads = vehicle X 2
- all building have income 100 / turn
- to start no building - 0 actions
- 0 actions for all objects. interactions will take place on people
- you must kill the castle to lose
- step on object to pick up / acquire (?) money is the only object for now 
- step on building not allowed
- keep movement and action separate
- attack matrices: attacker has advantage 1.2 : 1, health by damage, all units do even damage (no defense)

# not MVP
- stand next to unoccupied unit to capture (all will be pre-captured for now)
